# CampusMind University: Residence Hall & Mess Guidelines Handbook

## 1. Hostel Accommodation & Facilities
The university provides on-campus residential facilities across 4 student blocks:
- Aryabhatta Hall (Boys Senior Hostel)
- Ramanujan Hall (Boys Junior Hostel)
- Kalpana Chawla Hall (Girls Hostel Block A)
- Gargi Hall (Girls Hostel Block B)

Room options include Single Occupancy (Air-Conditioned), Double Sharing (AC/Non-AC), and Triple Sharing. Every room includes a study table, ergonomic chair, wardrobe, and high-speed campus Wi-Fi (100 Mbps per node).

## 2. Hostel Curfew Timings & Attendance
- General Campus Entry Deadline: All students must enter the main campus gates by 9:30 PM.
- Hostel In-time / Curfew Time: All resident students must mark biometric attendance inside their designated hostel block by **10:00 PM** on weekdays (Monday to Friday) and by **10:30 PM** on weekends and public holidays.
- Late Night Passes: Students requiring library access or lab work past 10:00 PM must obtain a Night Pass signed by their Head of Department (HOD) and Hostel Warden at least 6 hours in advance.
- Night Out / Leave Request: Outstation leave requests must be submitted through the student portal 24 hours in advance with parent/guardian SMS authorization.

## 3. Mess Timings & Dining Regulations
The Central Dining Hall provides hygienic vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals 4 times a day:
- Breakfast: 7:30 AM – 9:15 AM
- Lunch: 12:15 PM – 2:00 PM
- Evening High Tea & Snacks: 4:45 PM – 6:00 PM
- Dinner: 7:45 PM – 9:30 PM
Special festive menus are arranged on the first Sunday of every month. Outside food delivery (Zomato/Swiggy) is permitted up to 9:00 PM at designated security gates.

## 4. Hostel Fee Structure (Per Semester)
- Triple Sharing (Non-AC): Rs. 35,000 + Mess Fee Rs. 22,000
- Double Sharing (AC): Rs. 50,000 + Mess Fee Rs. 22,000
- Single Occupancy (AC): Rs. 70,000 + Mess Fee Rs. 22,000
- Refundable Hostel Caution Deposit: Rs. 10,000.
