# CampusMind University: Scholarships & Financial Assistance Schemes

## 1. Merit-Based Entrance Scholarships
CampusMind University awards generous tuition waivers to high-achieving applicants:
- **Chancellor's Gold Scholarship (100% Tuition Fee Waiver):** Awarded to students with JEE Main All India Rank (AIR) < 5,000 or CBSE/State Board aggregate >= 98%.
- **Dean's Merit Scholarship (50% Tuition Fee Waiver):** Awarded to students with JEE Main AIR 5,001 – 15,000 or 10+2 marks >= 94%.
- **Academic Excellence Award (25% Tuition Fee Waiver):** Awarded to students with 10+2 marks between 90% – 93.9%.
Renewal of merit scholarships in subsequent years requires maintaining a minimum SGPA of 8.5 with no disciplinary record.

## 2. Means-cum-Merit Financial Aid
- Students whose total annual family income from all sources is less than Rs. 3,50,000 per annum are eligible for up to 75% tuition assistance and subsidized mess charges.
- Applications open in September every year via the Student Welfare Office.

## 3. Sports & Cultural Achievements Scholarship
- National Level Sports Medalists / Participants: 50% to 100% sports scholarship plus free on-campus gymnasium and sports kit sponsorship.
