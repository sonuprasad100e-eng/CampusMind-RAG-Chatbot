# CampusMind University: Career Development & Placement Report 2025-2026

## 1. Placement Highlights & Statistics
The Centre for Corporate Relations (CCR) achieved phenomenal placement milestones for the graduating batch of 2025-2026:
- Total Placement Percentage: 94.8% eligible students placed.
- Highest International Package: Rs. 54.0 LPA (Offered by Tokyo Tech Labs).
- Highest Domestic Package: Rs. 42.5 LPA (Offered by Microsoft & Google).
- Average Package (CSE / AI): Rs. 14.8 LPA.
- Overall Campus Average Package: Rs. 9.6 LPA.
- Median Package: Rs. 8.2 LPA.
- Total Recruiters Visited: 240+ global corporations and top-tier startups.

## 2. Top Recruiting Partners
- Technology & Cloud: Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Oracle, Adobe, Cisco, Atlassian, Infosys, TCS Ninja/Digital.
- Financial & Fintech: Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JP Morgan Chase, DE Shaw, Paytm.
- Consulting & Analytics: McKinsey & Company, Deloitte, PwC, EY, Fractal Analytics.

## 3. Mandatory Summer Internship Policy
- All 3rd Year (6th Semester) B.Tech students must complete a mandatory 8-to-10 week corporate or research internship during summer break (June - July).
- Average Internship Stipend: Rs. 45,000 per month.
- Highest Internship Stipend: Rs. 1,20,000 per month (Uber & Rubrik).

## 4. Placement Eligibility & Dream Offer Policy
- Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.5 with no active backlogs to register with CCR.
- "One Student, One Job" Policy: Once a student receives an offer between 4-8 LPA, they are eligible to apply for "Dream Offers" (>= 12 LPA) and "Super Dream Offers" (>= 20 LPA).
