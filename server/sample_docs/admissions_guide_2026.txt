# CampusMind University: Official Admissions & Fee Guide 2026-2027

## 1. Undergraduate Programs Offered & Eligibility Criteria
CampusMind University offers world-class undergraduate degree programs across diverse engineering and applied science disciplines:

- **Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE):**
  - Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
  - Eligibility: Minimum 75% aggregate in 10+2 (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics) or equivalent from a recognized board, along with a valid JEE Main score or CUET (UG) percentile of 85+.
  - Total Seats: 180 seats.

- **Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE):**
  - Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
  - Eligibility: Minimum 70% aggregate in 10+2 with PCM.

- **Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Mechanical & Robotics Engineering:**
  - Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
  - Eligibility: Minimum 65% aggregate in 10+2 with PCM.

- **Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science:**
  - Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  - Eligibility: B.Tech/B.E. in relevant discipline with minimum 60% or 6.5 CGPA and valid GATE score.

## 2. Annual Tuition & Academic Fee Structure (2026-2027)
- B.Tech (CSE / AI): Rs. 1,85,000 per academic year (payable in two semester installments of Rs. 92,500).
- B.Tech (ECE / Mech): Rs. 1,50,000 per academic year.
- M.Tech Programs: Rs. 1,20,000 per academic year.
- One-time Security Deposit (Refundable at graduation): Rs. 15,000.
- One-time Admission Processing Fee (Non-refundable): Rs. 5,000.

## 3. Admission Timeline & Key Deadlines
- Application Portal Opens: February 15, 2026
- Phase 1 Application Deadline: May 30, 2026
- Counseling and Seat Allocation Round 1: June 15, 2026
- Orientation Day & Document Verification: August 1, 2026
- Commencement of Classes: August 10, 2026

## 4. Fee Refund Policy
If a candidate withdraws their admission:
- 15 days or more before the formally announced last date of admission: 100% refund of tuition fees (minus Rs. 1,000 processing fee).
- Less than 15 days before the last date: 90% refund.
- Up to 15 days after the last date: 80% refund.
- Between 16 to 30 days after the last date: 50% refund.
- More than 30 days after the formally announced last date: No tuition fee refund; security deposit is refunded.
