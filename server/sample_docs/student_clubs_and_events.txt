# CampusMind University: Student Life, Technical Clubs & Annual Festivals

## 1. Recognized Technical & Cultural Student Clubs
CampusMind features over 25 active student-led societies:
- **Codex Hackers Club:** Organizes weekly competitive programming jams, open-source workshops, and the 36-hour flagship HackMind Hackathon.
- **AeroMind & Robotics Society:** Designs autonomous drones, rover bots, and participates in the University Rover Challenge.
- **Rhythm & Soul (Music & Dance Society):** Manages college band performances and cultural choreography.
- **Literary & Debating Society (LitDeb):** Hosts parliamentary debates, Model United Nations (MUN), and poetry slams.
- **GreenEarth Eco Club:** Leads on-campus sustainability, tree plantation, and e-waste recycling campaigns.

## 2. Annual Campus Festivals & Events
- **InnovateX (National Technical Symposium):** Held annually in October. Features robotics combats, project expos, AI coding sprints, and guest lectures from industry leaders. Cash prizes exceed Rs. 5,00,000.
- **Tarang (Annual Cultural Fest):** 3-day cultural extravaganza held in February featuring celebrity musical nights, EDM concerts, fashion shows, and street plays.
- **Spardha (Inter-College Sports Meet):** Conducted in January across 12 sporting disciplines including Football, Basketball, Cricket, Badminton, and Table Tennis.
